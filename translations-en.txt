小舟、分段行舟路线、环境声音与六处发现交互、六处观景提示是本网站的艺术演绎，并非原画动画或历史复原。环境声音默认关闭；卷首题诗可点击阅读简体全文。探索记录仅保存在当前浏览器。山水采用多点透视，水面不相接处以淡出转场；水磨示意与流水声音均为体验演绎。=The moving boat, local water routes, optional sound and twelve stops are an artistic interpretation, not a historical reconstruction. The boat sprite was derived with AI assistance from a painted boat. Sound is off by default; discoveries stay in this browser. Disconnected waterways use fade transitions. The watermill diagram and synthesized water sound are interpretive additions.
